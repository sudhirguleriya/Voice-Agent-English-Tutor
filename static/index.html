How to use it

  1. Press the mic and talk about anything: your day, your work, a plan.
  2. After each sentence you get the fix and a more natural way to say it.
  3. Press 🎙 Say it and repeat the natural version. Saying it yourself is what makes it stick.
  4. Open Practice each day. Your own mistakes come back as flashcards until you've mastered them.
A new conversation starts each time you turn on the mic, or after a 10-minute break.

Practise your own mistakes

You see a sentence you said wrong and say the correct version out loud. A correct answer pushes the card's next review further out (1 → 3 → 7 → 14 → 30 days). A wrong one brings it back tomorrow.
